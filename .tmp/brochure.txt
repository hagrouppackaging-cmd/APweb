297: SECTION 02 · REVENUE MIX VS PROFIT MIX
298: Admission is your best margin and your worst
299: growth lever. Both are true.
300: 
301: Admission income carries almost no cost of goods, so every pound of it is close to a pound of gross profit.
302: 
303: That makes it the most valuable pound in the building — and the most constrained. You collect it once per
304: 
305: visitor, it is capped by session capacity and fire limits, and it is the single most price-compared number you
306: 
307: publish. Secondary spend is the opposite: lower margin per pound, effectively uncapped, and almost entirely
308: 
309: within your control. Growth in a play centre is therefore a secondary-spend problem, not a footfall problem.
